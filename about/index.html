About the Show

It's Gonna Be a Problem is the relationships podcast for women who are done shrinking and done saying "it's fine" when it was not fine. Host Kendra Jade talks about dating, boundaries, and clear standards: red flags, situationships, mixed signals, and the moment you finally say "absolutely not."

This is brunch-truth. Bold, funny, and unfiltered, with warmth under the attitude. Every episode mixes real stories, listener drama, a little humor, and practical takeaways, including word-for-word scripts you can actually say out loud.

Season 1 is called From Easy to Clear. It walks you from the chill, low-maintenance, "no, it's fine" version of yourself to someone who can say what she wants out loud.

What the show is not: It is not a therapy session, a clinical advice line, or a shame spiral. It is not soft, generic "good vibes only" dating content. The goal is empowerment and self-respect, not drama for its own sake.

This show is explicit. Swearing and adult politics are part of the conversation, on purpose.

The house rules

  • No shrinking.
  • No apologizing for your standards.
  • Petty is allowed. Cruel is not.
  • We laugh, but we grow.
  • People do not give you what you deserve. They give you what you accept.
