Your story stays yours

On the show, names are changed, identifying details are swapped, and I never read your email address, handle, or anyone's real name. If you say "just for Kendra," it stays just for me. You can ask me to pull your story any time before it airs. You must be 18 or older to submit. Sending a story does not guarantee it will be used on the show.

What to send

Email stories@itsgonnabeaproblem.com with:

  1. What should we call you on air? A fake name is great.
  2. Your situation, in your own words. What happened, and what you're stuck on.
  3. What do you want from Kendra? A Petty or Peace? verdict, a script for what to say, a gut check, or you just need to vent.
  4. Can Kendra read this on the show? Yes, with details changed; yes, but summarize it; or no, just for Kendra.
  5. Confirm you are 18 or older and that you understand the story may be edited, with details changed, to protect everyone involved.

Please do not include full legal names, the other person's name, workplaces, exact cities, social handles, or photos of other people. Voice memos are welcome as an email attachment.

If anything you want to write involves someone threatening you, controlling you, or hurting you, please don't wait on a podcast. Call or text the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, text START to 88788, or visit thehotline.org. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.
