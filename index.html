Meet the host

Kendra Jade hosts It's Gonna Be a Problem: brunch-truth about dating, boundaries, and standards. Bold, funny, and unfiltered, with warmth under the attitude.

About the show

What you'll get

Not vibes. Words. A relationships podcast with real talk, a little humor, and a practical takeaway you can use the same night.

The Script

Word-for-word lines for the hard conversations: "that doesn't work for me," the "what are we" talk, and calling out a red flag without starting a war.

Petty or Peace?

Verdicts on messy dating situations. Petty is allowed. Cruel is not. You leave with a clear call, not a lecture.

Real talk

Red flags in dating, situationships, mixed signals, and the moment you finally say "absolutely not." Listener identities stay protected.
