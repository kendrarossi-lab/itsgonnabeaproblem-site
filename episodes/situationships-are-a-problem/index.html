About this episode

A situationship is the relationship that isn't a relationship: no label, a lot of feelings, and a toothbrush with no name for what you are. In this episode, Kendra Jade gets honest about undefined dating, mixed signals, and why "just seeing where it goes" can keep you stuck for months.

This is not a lecture for people who are undefined on purpose and happy about it. It is for the ones who want more and are scared to ask. You get That's Gonna Be a Problem on situationship stories (sample scenarios, clearly labeled), the exact words for the "what are we" talk, and how to walk away if the answer is "I don't know."

"I don't know" is an answer. "Not right now" for a year is just "no" with good manners. Set your own timeline before someone else sets it for you.

Listener takeaway: Decide your timeline before someone else decides it for you. "I don't know" is an answer, and you are allowed to leave it.
