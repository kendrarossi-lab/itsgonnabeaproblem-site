Dating Standards: Why I'm Done Being Easy

I used to be the low-maintenance girlfriend. Then I realized low maintenance just meant nobody had to try.

About this episode

Dating standards start here. In the first episode of It's Gonna Be a Problem, Kendra Jade explains why being the "easy," low-maintenance girlfriend in dating cost more than it ever saved, and why she is done with the bare minimum. "Chill" gets rewarded with less effort, not more love. The girl who never asks for anything ends up with exactly that.

This is the origin of the show and the house rules. No shrinking. No apologizing for your standards. Petty is allowed, cruel is not. The lesson is blunt: people do not give you what you deserve. They give you what you accept. Easy was never the flex. Clear is the flex.

You also get The Script, word-for-word lines for saying "that doesn't work for me" without a TED Talk, the first round of Petty or Peace?, and one standard to raise this week: stop saying "it's fine" when it isn't. If you have been the understanding one for too long, start here.

Listener takeaway: Stop saying "it's fine" when it isn't. "That doesn't work for me" is a complete sentence.
