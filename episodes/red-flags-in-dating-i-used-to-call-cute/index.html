About this episode

Red flags in dating do not get less red because you like the person holding them. In this episode, Kendra Jade counts down the warning signs we call cute until they cost us: love bombing, "I'm just bad at texting," "all my exes are crazy," a temper we renamed passion, and the "private" person who is actually hiding you.

You saw the flag. You put a bow on it. Then three months later you were asking how you missed it. You didn't miss it. You dressed it up. Kendra translates five classics, plays a big Petty or Peace? round, and gives you the exact words for calling out a red flag on a date without starting a war.

Three times is a pattern. Private means they don't post you. Hidden means they don't let you in. Learn the difference, then stop painting the flag pink.

Listener takeaway: Name the flag out loud. Three times is a pattern. "I'm not overthinking it. I'm noticing it."
