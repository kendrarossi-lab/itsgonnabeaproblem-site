Straight answers about It's Gonna Be a Problem, the relationships podcast hosted by Kendra Jade: launch day, where to listen, stories, and what the show is (and is not).

What is It's Gonna Be a Problem?

It's Gonna Be a Problem is a relationships podcast about dating, boundaries, and standards, hosted by Kendra Jade. It is bold, funny, unfiltered talk for women who are done shrinking, with word-for-word scripts you can use the same night. The tagline is BOUNDARIES ARE HOT.

Who hosts the show?

Kendra Jade hosts the show. On Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Threads, the official handle is @itsgonnabeaproblempod. On X, it is @kendrasproblem.

When does the show launch?

The show launches on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, with the trailer plus Season 1, Episodes 1 through 3. New episodes drop every Tuesday after that.

Where can I listen?

The show will be on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, and YouTube. Official listen buttons stay marked Coming Dec 1 until those listings are live. The YouTube channel is already at @itsgonnabeaproblempod. See the listen page for updates.

Is the show explicit?

Yes. Swearing and adult politics are part of the conversation, on purpose.

How do I send a story?

Email stories@itsgonnabeaproblem.com. You must be 18 or older. Names are changed, always, and identifying details are swapped. Sending a story does not guarantee it will be used. Read the story guide before you write.

What is The Script?

The Script is a word-for-word line you can actually say out loud: for "that doesn't work for me," the "what are we" talk, or calling out a red flag without starting a war.

What is Petty or Peace?

Petty or Peace? is a verdict on a messy dating situation. Petty is allowed. Cruel is not. You leave with a clear call, not a lecture.

Is this therapy or clinical advice?

No. The show is not a therapy session, a clinical advice line, or a shame spiral. It is brunch-truth and practical takeaways. If you are in danger, call 911, or use the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, text START to 88788, or visit thehotline.org.

How do I contact the show?

Use hello@itsgonnabeaproblem.com for general questions, booking@itsgonnabeaproblem.com for guests and press, and stories@itsgonnabeaproblem.com for listener submissions. There is no personal phone number or personal Gmail on this site.

Will there be merch?

Yes, later. The shop page is a placeholder until official merch is ready. Nothing is for sale there yet. Email hello@itsgonnabeaproblem.com if you want a heads-up when the shop opens.

What is Season 1 about?

Season 1 is called From Easy to Clear. It walks you from the chill, low-maintenance, "no, it's fine" version of yourself to someone who can say what she wants out loud. Season 2, Keep It or Quit It, will follow once Season 1 is out.
